## Problem

Most kids' apps lean on passive consumption or paywalled mechanics. I wanted something my daughter could play that rewarded creativity and slipped in a little learning without feeling like homework.

## Solution

Built a SwiftUI app where kids design unicorns with deep customization, then unlock premium decorations, wings, and backgrounds by completing age-appropriate math, counting, and ordering challenges. Added a photo studio, gallery, daily rewards, haptics, and full VoiceOver/Dynamic Type accessibility.

## Outcome

A kid-friendly, fully local (no accounts, no ads) iOS prototype that balances creative play with gentle skill-building.
