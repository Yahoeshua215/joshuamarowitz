## Problem

Marketers need to design cross-channel customer journeys quickly, but starting from a blank canvas in a complex product is slow and intimidating.

## Solution

Prototyped an intelligent journey builder landing experience with AI-powered suggestions, multi-channel workflow framing, and analytics hooks — deployed as a v0 concept on Vercel for brainstorming and stakeholder review.

## Outcome

A shareable concept site that communicates the vision for AI-assisted journey creation before committing to in-product implementation.
