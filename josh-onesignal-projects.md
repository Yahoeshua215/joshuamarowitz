## How this was built

I searched every project-prefixed channel in the OneSignal workspace (`proj-*`, `project-*`, public **and** private, including archived) and then confirmed Josh's involvement two ways:

1. **Channels Josh created** — definite membership.
2. **Channels where Josh posted** — verified by searching his own messages in each channel.

Channels that exist in the workspace but where Josh never created or posted (e.g. `proj-oauth`, `proj-clickhouse-migration`, `proj-counters-idempotency`, `proj-fix-redis-billing-issues`, `proj-pgcat-for-rust`, and many other backend/infra channels) were **excluded**.

> Note: Slack's API doesn't expose passive membership directly, so "been a part of" here means **created or actively posted in**. If Josh was a silent member of any additional channel, it wouldn't show up — but for project involvement, participation is the meaningful signal.
